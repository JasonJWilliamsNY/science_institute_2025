Science Institute Lab Safety Rules

These rules are adapted for the Science Institute. Depending on the work and the materials, some laboratory environments may have additional specific rules.

  1. Do not eat, drink, or chew gum in the lab. Avoid hand-to-mouth activities such as nail biting or chewing on the end of a pencil/pen. Keep food and drink secure and away from lab spaces. Note: You may ask permission to leave the lab space at any time to have a drink or a quick snack if needed. Wash your hands after lab sessions.

  2. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment as instructed during lab sessions. This may include goggles, lab coats, and gloves. Shoes should be closed-toed, and loose clothing or hair should be secured, especially if a flame is in use.

  3. Students must never be in the laboratory space alone, or perform experiments alone and/or unsupervised. Even during lecture sessions, please wait outside the lab until both students and the instructor are present.

  4. Do not smell or taste any chemical unless specifically instructed (e.g., during the "tasting" lab).

  5. Read and reread lab protocols to identify hazards. If you are uncertain about how to perform an operation safely, ask for help. Pay attention and remain focused on your work during lab sessions.

  6. Report unsafe behavior, hazards, or injuries. It is better to mention a possible concern than to ignore it — if you see something, say something.
