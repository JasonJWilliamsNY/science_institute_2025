Quiz Review Sheet

Quiz: Mid-year review

Ground rules/instructions

  1. Virtual quiz - instructions provided in email
  2. This quiz is intended to test some standard concepts we have covered over the past year, and correct misunderstandings or reinforce correct understandings.

Review

The following will be important to review for this quiz, use these when considering the example topics/questions below:

  • Please use all of the notes, slides, and classroom discussion posted previously as a basis. This quiz will NOT test the upcoming GMO materials. You should also look up any additional sources needed to secure your understanding.

Here are the important topics/questions you should know for the quiz (Note: We will not cover DNA sequencing on this quiz).

From our lab safety and measurement discussions

  • Why is it important to review lab safety protocols, even though we have done so many months ago?
  • What is the reasoning behind our prohibition on consuming food and drink in the lab?

From our basic DNA, mitochondiral sequencing, and bacterial genetics discussions

  • When we perform a DNA extraction, what is the purpose of the various steps (e.g. heating cells, centrifuging cells, adding chelex)?
  • What is a plasmid?
  • What do restriction enzymes do?
  • What is 'recombinant' DNA?

From our PCR and electrophoresis discussions

  • What the stages of the PCR process are and what happens at each stage (e.g. Denature, anneal, extend)
  • What the chemical components of PCR are (e.g. polymerase, primer, template DNA, etc.) and what their purpose is.
  • What is the purpose of using electrophoresis?
  • What happens when we raise or lower the percentage of agarose in a gel?

From our PTC lab and experimental control discussions

  • What is the gene responsible for bitter taste perception? What does the protein do?
  • What is an experimental control and how do we use it in the PTC lab?

From our writing and communication discussions

  • Why do scientists need to write effectively?
  • What are the steps of the 'paramedic method' of editing?
