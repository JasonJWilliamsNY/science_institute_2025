Quiz Review Sheet

Quiz: 6 DNA Barcoding

Ground rules/instructions

  1. Virtual quiz - instructions provided in email.
  2. This quiz is intended to test some standard concepts we have covered over the past year, and correct misunderstandings or reinforce correct understandings.

Review

The following will be important to review for this quiz, use these when considering the example topics/questions below:

Please use all of the notes, slides, and classroom discussion posted previously as a basis.

  • DNA Barcoding slides
  • DNA Barcoding protocol packet

What to study: The following topics will be covered on the quiz. You should be able to:

  • Explain the basic steps of DNA Barcoding (Collection, DNA Isolation, Sequencing, Identification) See details: http://www.dnabarcoding101.org/dna_barcoding_ani.html
  • Be able to explain the steps of DNA Isolation using the protocol in class, including what the function of each step is (e.g. Grinding in Lysis Buffer is to break up the cell so that DNA can be isolated by the subsequent steps) See the details here: http://www.dnabarcoding101.org/protocol_isolating_dna.html#standard
  • Know which genes are used to barcode what types of organisims.
  • Understand the differences between coding and non-coding DNA as explained in class. Note why some regions of DNA are good (or bad) to use as barcoding regions.
  • Be able to contract barcoding with traditional taxonomic approaches.
  • Understand how DNA Subway and other computer software can help us analyze, barcodes. Especially the function of BLAST.

All of these items were covered (to varying levels) in class. You can use your notes, each other, and any other books or online resources. Spending about 30-40 minutes to consolidate notes and research into a study sheet (you can actually do this in your class notebook) should be sufficient preparation for the quiz. Finding a few minutes to discuss with your peers will tell you if you are understanding things on the same level as everyone else.
