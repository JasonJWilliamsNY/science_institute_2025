Mitochondiral DNA analysis

Sequencing of the mitochondrial d-loop/control region

In this protocol we will amplify the d-loop/control region of your mitochondrial DNA using polymerase chain reaction (PCR).

Materials

Background PDF - Mitocondrial sequencing

  • Saline solution (0.9% NaCl)
  • Sterile cotton swabs
  • 10% Chelex®, 100 µl
  • 15 ml test tube, polypropylene
  • Paper cup
  • 1.5 ml test tubes, polypropylene
  • 1 ml transfer pipet or
  • 100-1,000 µl micropipet and tip
  • Microcentrifuge
  • Thermal cycler

Steps

Cheek swab and Chelex DNA extraction

Developed at the DNA Learning Center | Copyright  Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory. Human Mitochondrial Sequencing

PROCEDURE

Part I: DNA Isolation by Swab

  1. Label a tube containing 500 μL 0.9% saline with a number, as assigned by your teacher. This way, your results will be anonymous. This number will be used throughout the protocol.
  2. Using a sterile cotton swab with a 6” wood handle, thoroughly swab the inside of your cheek for 30 seconds, turning the swab occasionally so that the entire surface of the swab has been used.
  3. Swirl the swab in the saline tube for 30 seconds, rotating the wood stick rapidly between your fingers so that the cheek cells fall off of the swab into the liquid.
  4. Discard the swab into a special container designated for saliva waste.
  5. Place your sample tube, together with other student samples, in a balanced configuration in a microcentrifuge, and spin for 1 minute.
  6. Carefully pour off supernatant into the saliva waste container. Be careful not to disturb the cell pellet at the bottom of the test tube. A small amount of saline will remain in the tube.
  7. Resuspend cells in remaining saline by flicking the base of the tube vigorously.
  8. Use a micropipette to withdraw 30 μL of cell suspension, and add cells to the tube containing 100 μL of Chelex. Shake well to mix.
  9. Boil cell sample for 10 minutes. Use boiling water bath, heat block, or program thermal cycler for 10 minutes at 99°C. Then, cool tube briefly on ice (optional).
  10. After boiling, shake tube. Place in a balanced configuration in a microcentrifuge, and spin for 1 minute.
  11. Use a micropipette to transfer 30 μL of supernatant (containing the DNA) to clean 1.5 mL tube. Avoid cell debris and Chelex beads. This sample will be used for setting up the PCR reaction.
  12. Store your sample on ice or in the freezer until ready to begin Part II.

D-loop/control region PCR amplification

Part II: PCR

  1. Use fresh tip to add 2.5 µl of human DNA (from Part I) to reaction tube, and tap to mix. Pool reagents by pulsing in a microcentrifuge or by sharply tapping tube bottom on lab bench.
  2. Label the cap of your tube with a number, as assigned by your teacher. In this way, your results will be anonymous.
  3. Store all samples on ice until ready to amplify according to the following profile. Program thermal cycler for 30 cycles according to the following cycle profiles. Each program may be linked to a 4°C to hold samples after completing the cycle profile, but amplified DNAs also hold well at room temperature.

PCR Protocol

  • Denaturing time and temperature 30 sec - 94°C
  • Annealing time and temperature 30 sec - 58°C
  • Extending time and temperature 30 sec - 72°C
  • Hold at -4°C (optional)
